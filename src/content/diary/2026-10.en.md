## 11

I haven't been in a great state lately. The Dingjiaqiao campus is tiny, so the walk to class is only a few minutes. Which means I've completely lost the listening-to-music time I used to have on the way to class at Sipailou.

Today the computer was running a training job and became so laggy it was unusable, so I couldn't do anything. I decided to catch up on systematic anatomy and histology and embryology. For the first time in half a year I put my headphones on and started studying.

I heard Yorushika's "Hana Ningen Goku", my favourite track to walk to; its gently unfolding bridge and perfect length compress the trip to an 8 a.m. class into the span of one song.
I heard "Free Bird". I heard "To the Nameless Fighters". Those were what I listened to on the way to the library to revise for the major-transfer exam. In my most doubtful and difficult moments they gave me courage and strength.
I heard some lo-fi hip hop from Asase Saki's playlist. They smoothed over every anxious, sleepless night.

What is that, exactly? I think it's a kind of "hate".
