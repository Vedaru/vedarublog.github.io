## 07

Oh my god.

neovim + tmux + WezTerm is the perfect workflow. I've set WezTerm to drop straight into WSL2, then use tmux inside for terminal switching: one session runs Hermes, the others let me hop between projects.

It's genuinely great. I went from Cursor taking half a minute to start, to being able to fly inside neovim within one or two seconds. And if I want to delegate something to Hermes, it's just ctrl+a+s to open the sessions panel and switch over.
It's amazing, like driving an automatic for years and finally getting into a manual drift car. Woohoo~

## 26

I replaced Windows with Linux, because I realised:
Krita, Kdenlive, Blender, Wine. Turning a free system into what it is today was never the big vendors' mountains of legacy code and data harvesting; it's the love of everyone in the community pushing back against the weight of the world.
Yet some people feed a criminal capital with their own privacy and then have the nerve to mock Linux for lacking a big-vendor ecosystem. How absurd.
The open-source community is arguably the largest collective technological awakening in human history, the closest thing to communism on both a technical and a spiritual level. It deserves its own chapter in future history books.
That said, I don't hate people who use Windows or macOS, because their existence is why the big vendors exist, and because the big vendors exist, our anger has something to push against.
It's a complicated relationship. I don't look back in anger, but I don't thank the pain either.
